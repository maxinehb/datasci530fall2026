OpenAlex

OpenAlex is a free database of research publications where one can get information such as a work’s title, publication year, citation count, authors, and their affiliated institutions. We will be using its API to search for works by title and compare the results. An API lets my Python code request information from a website.

Summary of Findings

Here are the main findings:

  • There are more than 330,000 works with "artificial intelligence" in the title on OpenAlex

  • Since 2000, the United States, China, and India had the highest counts by authors’ affiliation countries

  • Title counts increased substantially over time. “Artificial intelligence” was growing before 2023, while titles containing “LLM” and “GPT” rose especially sharply starting in 2023. “AI” appeared in the most titles by 2026.

  • Among the ten most cited 2026 articles for each of the terms “artificial intelligence” and “AI,” the two searches produced different top ten lists with fairly similar citation counts.

terms = ["artificial intelligence", "LLM", "GPT", "AI"]

Comparing this to the use of the terms "LLM" and "GPT", these terms were not used as much as "artificial intelligence" in publication titles from 2000-2022. In 2023, both terms were used over 1,000 times in titles, and "GPT" usage in titles has not increased much since then compared to "LLM" exponentially increasing to 10,865 in 2024 and 52,628 in 2026 so far.

Comparing to the keyword "AI", the term has exponentially increased in title appearances the same time that "artificial intellegence" became a more popular phrase, which makes sense as "AI" is the abbreviation for "artificial intelligence." Yet, "AI" is seen in titles far more than its equivalent phrase "artificial intelligence," surpassing it in 2024 in 62,651 titles and the number of titles with "AI" in 2026 so far is 256,642.

Thus, "AI" is the most popular term used of the four and has exponentially grown in usage, especially this year, with "artificial intelligence" following behind it with much less frequcency but still a significant amount. "LLM" is used half as much as "artificial intelligence," and "GPT" remains an unpopular phrase in titles to-date.

# find ten most cited 2026 articles for each title term
terms_q5 = ["artificial intelligence", "AI"]
tables_q5 = {}

for term in terms_q5:
    url_q5 = (
        f'https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=title.search:"{term}",'
        'type:article,'
        'from_publication_date:2026-01-01,'
        'to_publication_date:2026-09-27'
        '&sort=cited_by_count:desc'
    )

    search_results_q5 = requests.get(url_q5).json()
    top10_q5 = search_results_q5["results"][:10]

    # quality check:
    #print(term, len(top10_q5))

    rows_q5 = []

    for rank, work in enumerate(top10_q5, start=1):
        country_codes = []
        country_text = ""

        for authorship in work["authorships"]:
            for institution in authorship["institutions"]:
                country_code = institution["country_code"]

                
                if country_code is not None and country_code not in country_codes:
                    country_codes.append(country_code)

            "Rank": rank,
